Delete your ER Time account and data

ER Time (ER Wait Time) is operated by Innovators Generation. If you created an account in the app, you can request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@innovatorsgeneration.com from the email address linked to your account.
  2. Use subject line: Delete ER Time account and data.
  3. We will confirm your identity and delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

What we delete

When your request is approved, we delete account credentials, profile information, saved preferences, and other personal data linked to your ER Time account.

Data that may be retained

We may retain anonymized analytics or records required for legal, security, or fraud-prevention purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.
